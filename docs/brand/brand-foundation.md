# Brand foundation

**Brand spelling:** Tavistra (T-A-V-I-S-T-R-A).  
**Relationship:** premium lifestyle/décor sub-brand of VDesign, the core premium custom-furniture business (vdesign.life). VDesign remains the furniture/site-integrated business; Tavistra targets design-led standalone lifestyle and décor objects.

## Positioning — agreed baseline
Premium, design-led, handcrafted or curated objects with artistic identity and practical use where appropriate. D2C-oriented, premium positioning rather than competing on lowest price. Original designs, limited runs, curated third-party/private-label options may all be considered. Primary initial materials often wood/plywood/MDF and engineered wood; reclaimed tech components are an optional design language, not the whole brand. Small batches generally <=10 pieces per design/variant/batch are a planning baseline, expandable when demand warrants. In-house design/production preferred where feasible; components or complete production may be outsourced; warranty/service remain brand responsibilities.

## Boundary with VDesign
- **Tavistra:** freestanding or simply mounted objects requiring no major on-site installation.
- **VDesign:** built-in furniture, dressers/vanities, extensive installation, architectural cladding, permanent structures, site-integrated work and custom furniture.
- Borderline examples: simply mounted wooden-frame wall mirror can be Tavistra; dresser with integrated mirror belongs to VDesign.

## Product and compliance policy
Initially avoid product families that create onerous new BIS, electrical, child safety, food-contact or other regulatory obligations without a robust compliant supply route. Lighting and similar products remain design candidates, **not automatically approved for manufacture**. A certified component/vendor does not necessarily establish compliance of a private-labelled finished good. Assess legal requirements, safety, warranty and certification for individual SKU routes.

## Discovery layers
Main product categories are mutually distinct by primary intent; meaningful overlaps allowed beneath them. Collections/tags may overlap categories: Gifting, Spiritual & Cultural, Kitchen & Dining, Festive & Seasonal, Reclaimed, Experimental, Limited Edition. These are **not** independent main categories. Gifting and festive are especially collection-oriented, not taxonomy branches.

## Removed directions
Conventional jewellery (rings, necklaces, bracelets, earrings); apparel/fashion-adjacent accessories; stationery, books and pens; travel/everyday carry as a standalone **category**; children's/electrical/food-contact categories without a safe compliance strategy; large outdoor utility/organisation and architectural outdoor installations. Historical ideas must remain discoverable in the decision register rather than resurrected by accident.
