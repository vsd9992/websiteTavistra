# Tavistra — pragmatic initial PIN-code launch policy

**Owner direction, 2026-11-06:** Approve a practical launch approach using major urban destination PIN codes supported by credible public postal and courier coverage information. Do not require perfect carrier-by-carrier validation of every PIN before launch. This supersedes earlier planning language requiring explicit carrier/fragile commodity acceptance for every single destination PIN as a prerequisite to launch; it does **not** waive product-specific transport feasibility or statutory/customer safeguards.

## Approved operating boundary
- Owner-approved individual six-digit PIN whitelist only; no blanket 3-digit-prefix or city-wide automatic enablement. Use reliable public postal evidence plus available public courier coverage signals. Distinguish postal validity from actual commodity-specific courier feasibility. Record source/date and approver; allow risk-based sampling and periodic corrections rather than perfection.
- Keep product-specific transport exclusions for oversized, unusually fragile, glass/mirror or otherwise restricted items; ordinary parcel coverage is not blanket commodity acceptance. Operations can select any of approved Delhi origins 110062, 110019, 110074, while the customer fee schedule stays origin-independent.
- If a confirmed order cannot be delivered, staff must check feasible alternate approved origin/carrier and communicate with customer. Do not unilaterally substitute material terms or demand a surprise extra delivery fee. If delivery is impossible, cancel the affected order and refund **100% of money actually collected for that cancelled order, including delivery charges**, without penalty; reconcile payment/refund status and notify customer. Preserve statutory rights and applicable provider payment timelines.
- Correct the exact affected PIN × product/shipping profile (or PIN globally if truly unavailable), retain reason, evidence, approver and audit; block new impacted orders pending review. Existing order records and purchase-time terms remain intact.
- Separate Ready Stock, Made to Order and Quote Only workflows; for MTO/Quote Only, discovery of infeasibility should be as early as practical and **before production release/actual start**, with a hold while staff review it. If detected after production start, resolve customer cancellation/refund obligations without assuming normal discretionary cancellation rules excuse a seller-side inability to fulfil.
- Delivery Fee remains one manually assigned customer charge per order, equal to highest applicable product fee for that destination; no live carrier rates. Baseline packing/freight risk remains in Product Price.

## 2026-11-06 — Owner-approved post-audit delivery / bulk-order reconciliation (CURRENT AUTHORITY)

This section takes precedence over inconsistent earlier shipping, PIN, tax-presentation and checkout details. No website implementation is authorised by this planning correction.

### Quantity boundary and bulk enquiries
- A normal purchasable order is allowed **up to 10 units of any single product** AND **up to 10 distinct order line items**. The two ceilings are independent: breaching **either** triggers the bulk path; exactly 10 is allowed. Aggregate identical products across repeated cart additions/lines to prevent accidental line-splitting bypass; variants are distinct order lines but the **10-per-product** maximum applies across that product's variants within one order. Quantity controls apply to the effective order being checked out, not an arbitrary combined count across independent Ready Stock / Made to Order / Quote Only commitments.
- If either ceiling is exceeded, **do not create or collect payment for an ordinary order**. Offer a **bulk-order enquiry** instead. The enquiry captures proposed product(s), quantity, shipping destination and customer contact; staff review negotiated product discounts, delivery feasibility, single agreed delivery charge and payment/production terms before issuing customer-accepted terms. Existing Quote Only product quoting remains its separate mode; a bulk enquiry is not automatically a confirmed, paid order. Staff can prepare an appropriately mode-specific quote/commitment after review. The ordinary fee ladder and highest-product-fee rule do **not** automatically price bulk transactions.

### Feasibility during seven-day production grace
- For Made to Order and Quote Only confirmed orders, staff use the existing **independent seven-calendar-day no-production-start period** for a practical destination/packed-size/courier feasibility check. No guaranteed booking or all-PIN × product certification is required. **Unresolved material delivery infeasibility blocks actual physical production start** until resolved; reaching day eight never automatically releases that block. Where delivery cannot be arranged, inform customer and apply seller-side cancellation/full-refund policy without a customer penalty. If found after production start, protect customer remedies and resolve operational loss internally. Existing payment verification, production permissions, grace, changes and cancellation protections remain unchanged.

### Fixed order delivery charge and partial cancellations
- Owner policy: **the accepted order delivery fee is fixed and is not routinely recalculated/refunded solely because one of several order items is partially cancelled**. Record it as an accepted order-level charge and preserve the original accepted pricing snapshot. No new amount is added unexpectedly. This does **not** override applicable legal refunds, customer remedies, full seller-side non-fulfilment refunds, or a specific revised agreement; an entirely cancelled order due to Tavistra's delivery inability gets a full refund including delivery fees. Staff must be able to issue legally required adjustments where applicable.

### Tax-inclusive final amount, not an assertion of GST exemption
- The ₹0/250/500/750/1,000/1,500/2,000/2,500/3,000/4,000/5,000/7,500 delivery fee values are **fixed final customer-payable amounts**. **Do not add GST, courier fees or other extras on top** at checkout or collection. GST/tax treatment, where legally applicable, is calculated **within** the accepted fee/transaction total and correctly reflected in statutory documents. The owner does **not** intend to sell a standalone logistics service; however that does **not** establish shipping fees are GST-exempt. Under CGST Act s.15(2)(c), incidental charges and amounts charged by a supplier in respect of delivery may be included in taxable supply value. Tax classification, blended/multiple HSN tax rates, rounding, invoices and credit notes need professional review before live trading. See https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-act/1000284/1000001 .

### Universal enabled-PIN eligibility, serviceability mistakes and missing rates
- Any owner-enabled six-digit PIN is **presumed available for all normal products by default**; no required product-by-PIN exclusions, blanket fragility exceptions or per-route carrier pre-certification. Staff handle unexpected infeasibility, contact customer and try alternate practical carriage. Disable/flag PIN **globally only if the destination itself is not generally workable**. For a one-off product/package issue, log an operational exception and review packaging/carrier rather than automatically delisting the whole PIN or requiring a SKU×PIN gate. If Tavistra cannot fulfil, cancel and fully refund affected **entire cancelled order** including the accepted delivery charge, subject to lawful partial-order obligations and any customer-agreed alternate disposition.
- Initial rate configuration is **development/catalogue work**, not an additional concept decision. Still, **a confirmed ordinary order must never accept payment with a missing/unknown fee or silently interpret an unset fee as ₹0**. Use the existing registered-but-unpriced manual estimate workflow or block purchase pending staff resolution. Product-specific fee per commercial destination band, 12 selectable slabs, packed-chargeable-weight benchmarking, customer two-component price structure, and normal **single highest applicable fee per independent order** remain in force.

### Explicit supersession
- Supersedes earlier **automatic product-specific PIN restrictions**, mandatory per-product carrier preapproval before launch, historical base-distance + weight/oversize calculation formula as the live checkout fee engine, and earlier suggestions to **recalculate/refund** delivery charges automatically after partial line cancellation.
- Earlier rate brackets, volumetric weight, oversize adjustments and route-distance logic are **internal costing/benchmark inputs only**. The displayed/charged ordinary fee is the approved **product × destination commercial band slab**, not a second formula or extra shipping line.
- Distinct Ready Stock, Made to Order and Quote Only payment/order/fulfilment paths, the verified 25% before production/100% before dispatch gates for MTO/Quote Only, independent 30-day original Quote Only validity and seven-day confirmed-order grace, customer legal rights and audit remain unchanged.
