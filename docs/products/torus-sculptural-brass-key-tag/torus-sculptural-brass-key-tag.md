# Torus Sculptural Brass Key Tag

Hand-finished lost-wax cast solid brass pocket talisman with screw-locking titanium wire.

## Full Description
Cast using the centuries-old lost wax method, the Torus Key Tag transforms an everyday utilitarian object into an heirloom pocket sculpture. Its generous chamfered profile sits comfortably in the palm or trouser pocket, gathering subtle marks that tell the story of its keeper's journeys.

A flexible Grade 5 aircraft-grade titanium wire with a knurled threaded brass barrel closure secures up to 8 standard domestic and vehicular keys without stretching or uncoupling under load.

## Highlights
- Lost-wax cast solid naval architectural brass
- Grade 5 aircraft titanium cable with knurled threaded closure
- Holds up to 8 keys securely with effortless hand-threaded access
- Chamfered tactile edge profile designed specifically for pocket comfort
- Living finish matures gracefully with everyday handle and wear

## Care & Maintenance
Clean occasionally with a dry soft cloth. Allow the brass to develop its natural golden patina, or restore bright highlights using a dab of brass polish.
