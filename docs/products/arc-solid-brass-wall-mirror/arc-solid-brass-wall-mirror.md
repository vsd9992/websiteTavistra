# Arc Sculptural Brass Wall Mirror

Hand-rolled architectural brass frame cradling high-definition distortion-free float glass.

## Full Description
Formed by hand using heavy-gauge architectural brass bar stock, the Arc Mirror pairs minimalist geometry with tactile warmth. The brass perimeter is carefully rolled, silver-soldered at the base joint, and given a directional satin brush before receiving a breathable wax coating.

The mirror glass is 6mm high-definition float glass with an anti-shatter backing membrane, backed by engineered moisture-resistant panelling and suspended via an interlocking aluminium Z-bar cleat system.

## Highlights
- Heavy-gauge continuous hand-rolled solid brass frame
- 6mm distortion-free high-definition float glass
- Integrated anti-shatter safety backing membrane
- Heavy-duty interlocking Z-bar wall cleat included for earthquake-safe mounting
- Available in Standard (600×900mm) and Grand Vanity (800×1200mm) editions

## Care & Maintenance
Clean glass surfaces with standard non-ammonia glass cleaner sprayed onto a microfiber cloth. Avoid spraying liquids directly into the brass frame channels. The brass will age gracefully; polish occasionally with fine jewelers wax to maintain the golden hue.
