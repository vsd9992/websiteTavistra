# Aether Architectural Desk Lamp

Counterbalanced articulated arm in gunmetal stainless steel with warm 2700K LED optic engine.

## Full Description
The Aether Desk Lamp combines fluid kinetic movement with precise optical engineering. Employing a calibrated solid brass counterweight suspended by a concealed high-tension cable system, the light head glides effortlessly to any position and holds its posture with zero spring rebound.

The luminaire head houses a custom 2700K architectural LED module featuring 95+ CRI colour rendering, eliminating eye fatigue during prolonged evening study or drafting sessions. An integrated rotary knurled dial enables continuous stepless dimming from 5% to 100%.

## Highlights
- Precision fluid counterbalance mechanism with zero spring fatigue
- High-CRI (95+) 2700K warm architectural illumination
- Smooth stepless knurled rotary dimmer switch on lamp head
- Heavy-gauge weighted circular base lined with dense silicone to eliminate desk marring
- Modular replaceable LED optic engine rated for 50,000 continuous hours

## Care & Maintenance
Wipe metal surfaces with a dry microfiber cloth. Avoid harsh chemical cleaners or silicone sprays around the kinetic pivot bearings.
