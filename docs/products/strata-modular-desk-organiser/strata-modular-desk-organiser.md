# Strata Modular Desk Organiser

Weighted cast composite stone with integrated anodised bronze pen channels.

## Full Description
Engineered to restore clarity to executive desks and creative drafting tables, the Strata Organiser anchors workspace essentials with deliberate mass. Cast in a dense high-density mineral aggregate weighing 1.8 kilograms, it resists movement when picking up pens or phones with one hand.

Precision-machined aluminium channels cradle pens at a resting angle, while a recessed basin lined with natural merino wool protects watches, rings, and reading glasses from scratches.

## Highlights
- Substantial 1.8kg weighted base prevents sliding across polished timber or glass
- Milled aluminium pen troughs with satin anodised finish
- German merino wool felt catchment protects small valuables
- Silicone vibration-damping feet concealed beneath base
- Delivered in a custom studio box with certificate of authenticity

## Care & Maintenance
Wipe stone body with a damp cloth. For the wool felt basin, use a lint roller or compressed air. Keep away from excessive moisture.
