# Phase 2 — Step 1: Ready Stock order and payment journey

**Status:** DRAFT functional architecture; standard checkout pre-payment stock reservation boundary OWNER-APPROVED (2026-11-01). Other boundaries remain open.
**Scope:** standard Ready Stock purchase only, plus a referenced exception for missing delivery pricing. Do not settle mixed-mode carts, Made to Order, Custom Enquiry or Quote Only here.
**Authority:** Phase 1 signed-off unified sitemap; WEB-004/005/018/019/038/040N, WEB-040AA–AJ, website requirements/architecture and latest LOCKED decision-register entries.

## 1. Conceptual journey

1. **Product discovery and selection:** customer views published Ready Stock offer, available options and indicative availability. Backend remains authoritative for selling price and actual stock. Cart entry exists, but its page/drawer interaction is WEB-038 DEFERRED.
2. **Order review and identity:** customer reviews intended Ready Stock lines and quantity, proceeds as guest or authenticated customer, and supplies delivery/contact information. Secure guest order recovery is required (WEB-005).
3. **Authoritative checkout validation:** backend rechecks published offer, stock, destination serviceability, current tax-inclusive discounted product amount and internal delivery charge. Customer sees **exactly two price components**: product price and one combined delivery charge if applicable (WEB-040N). Coupons remain OPEN; no invented coupon rules.
4. **Delivery price exception:** when an otherwise registered destination has no configured rate, customer can explicitly request manual review. Staff prepares and permissioned administrator approves; existing WEB-040AA–AJ rules create an unpaid order, reserve Ready Stock where available, send secure SMTP payment link, and cancel/release reservation on expiry. This is a **separate exception path**, not an automatic checkout order.
5. **Payment initiation:** standard checkout requests payment against server-authoritative amount/order context. Provider WEB-018 DEFERRED. Never mark an order paid based on browser redirect alone.
6. **Verification and confirmation:** backend verifies provider-signed payment evidence, amount and order association; idempotently updates authoritative payment/order state. Customer sees pending/failed/recoverable or verified confirmation and securely accessible order detail as appropriate.
7. **Fulfilment and follow-up:** Ready Stock reservation/allocation, staff courier assignment, dispatch/tracking, customer support and order-linked cancellation/return/refund requests are represented. Staff-entered tracking is not carrier-verified. Requests do not automatically change order or refund state.

## 3. Failure boundaries to resolve in Phase 2

- **Stale price or insufficient Ready Stock:** do not accept stale frontend totals or oversell; present revalidation result before payment.
- **Payment pending, duplicate callback, delayed webhook, interrupted browser return:** authoritative reconciliation and idempotent transitions, no false paid confirmation.
- **Payment succeeds while order/stock reservation update fails:** define recovery/reconciliation ownership and customer messaging before implementation.
- **Concurrent stock claims / reservation expiry:** specify timing and safe release/commit rules; existing manual-quote expiry rules remain authoritative for that path.
- **Delivery pricing unavailable:** do not silently invent a delivery charge; use locked customer-initiated manual review path when eligible.
- **Email failure:** cannot reverse order/payment/stock changes; manage visibility and retry without duplicate business effects.
- **Guest recovery / support access:** no order disclosure from order number and email alone; verified/expiring access required.
