## Direction audit principles
Separate meaningful development concepts (e.g., folding, rotating, kinetic, mechanical/puzzle) from shared visual languages (minimalist, geometric, sculptural, organic, architectural, reclaimed-tech) and product attributes (adjustable, compact, personalised, outdoor-suitable). Repeated terms across product families do not imply separate SKUs. Retain design diversity in Art & Collectibles even when constructions overlap.
