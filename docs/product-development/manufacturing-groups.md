# Manufacturing-alignment strategy

**Status:** Two-group principle accepted; proposed placement of individual types is **preliminary** and **not permanently locked**. The project owner paused product-selection work to design physical prototypes and a first batch.

## Group 1 — VDesign manufacturing-compatible
Objects where a complete viable manufacturing route uses VDesign's wood/ply/MDF, finishing, hand-machining, assembly, hardware and routine precision outsourcing, with low incremental equipment/process requirements. Purchased clock movements, mirror glass, magnets and hinges can be used if the overall finished good remains within established capabilities and applicable safety requirements.

**Provisional wood-based candidates**: wooden keychains/organisers/fobs, compact wooden cases, wooden clocks/frames/mirrors/panels/hangings/boxes/trays/racks/vases with suitable inserts, desk organisers/stands/paperweights/signs, adequately protected outdoor plant stands/wall décor, sculptures and art panels/objects made mainly by woodworking.

## Group 2 — Concept/design-compatible, manufacturing-different
Tavistra-aligned products that need distinct primary material, processes, specialist fabrication, integration or product-safety/compliance routes: conventional leather wallets, stamped/spring metal money clips, metal wearables, ceramic/glass vessels, fully electrical lamps, certain all-weather garden designs and metal/cast/kinetic objects. Outsourcing/vendor partnerships are acceptable, but not treated as equivalent to in-house capability.

## Important nuance
Classify **an individual material-specific concept**, not a whole taxonomy type forever. A wooden vase with purchased glass liner can be G1, ceramic vase G2. A wooden lamp base can be made using G1 resources, while the finished electrical lamp is G2 until a compliant integration route exists. A rigid wood card case can be G1; a leather wallet G2. Environmental durability (sun, moisture, hardware corrosion, attachment safety) must be substantiated. Both groups remain eligible for exploration; G1 is the natural first prototyping priority.

## Decision status
The earlier approximate 30/13 split was an illustrative type-level estimate, **not** an approved SKU allocation. Do not use it as a production gate. First-batch products, costings, lead times and batch quantities TBD.
